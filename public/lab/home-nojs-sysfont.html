Classic comfort, with a twist

Honey & Haven is a neighbourhood coffee shop on The Parade in Oadby: a calm, cosy spot to slow down, catch up with friends or get a little work done.

Alongside the classics, we’re known for Spanish lattes, Vietnamese egg coffee and matcha made nine ways. Ask about the secret menu, where past specials like the Popcorn Matcha and the Pistachio Spanish Latte live on.

  • Hot or iced. Every espresso drink, made the way you like it.
  • A secret menu. Past specials you still love, available on request.
  • Our own honey. Pure Honey made with Tythe Bees in the Vale of Belvoir.
